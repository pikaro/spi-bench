# Current link validation notes (2026-05-05)

After reseating boards/cables and uploading current firmware, the healthy SPI legs are master low-speed SPI <-> media and master high-speed SPI <-> gpu0. Both converge in master-first and slave-first reset orders; media publishes synthetic Beat at ~100/s and gpu0 receives ~97-100/s with no PubSub runner death. The high-speed router still reports gpu1 as dead with the intended config: master GPU1 CS GPIO11/ATT GPIO10 and gpu1 local MOSI GPIO9/MISO GPIO8/SCLK GPIO7/CS GPIO6/ATT GPIO5. Probes with gpu1 local CS7/SCLK6 and MOSI4/10/11/12/13 produced slave transaction completions but RX hello bytes stayed `00 00`, so do not leave probe configs in `src/gpu/config.hpp` without fresh proof. RS485 master<->io remains electrically/config silent: master repeats handshake retries, master and io RS485 stats stay txQ/rxQ 0, and reset order does not change it. Treat current gpu1/RS485 failures as link/pin/physical validation blockers, not PubSub/SPI CPU or queue pressure.
